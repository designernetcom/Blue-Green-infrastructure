LANDSCAPES WITH PURPOSE01 — 08
01

Landscape & horticulture

Living environments rooted in soil science. From native planting and specimen trees to expansive lawns, we create landscapes designed to flourish.

SOFTSCAPE · SOIL HEALTH · HORTICULTURE
02

Smart irrigation & automation

Precisely engineered drip and sprinkler networks, with weather-responsive controllers that deliver water where and when it is needed.

MICRO-IRRIGATION · SENSORS · CENTRAL CONTROL
03

Fountains & water features

Reflecting pools, water curtains and dynamic fountains. Hydraulic precision meets architectural intent, with integrated lighting, filtration and controls.

FOUNTAINS · WATER WALLS · LIGHT & MOTION
04

Lake & pond management

Integrated aeration, filtration and ecological restoration for lakes, ponds and reservoirs, supporting clearer water and balanced aquatic environments.

AERATION · FILTRATION · ECOLOGICAL RESTORATION
05

Hardscape & civil engineering

Site grading, stone pathways, retaining structures and storm-water networks. The essential engineering that makes a landscape resilient.

CIVIL WORKS · PAVING · DRAINAGE
06

Play & outdoor recreation

Thoughtfully planned outdoor play environments for communities, schools and public parks, with age-appropriate equipment and safety surfacing.

PLAY ENVIRONMENTS · RECREATION · SAFETY SURFACING
07

Green technology & solar

Solar pumping and controlled-environment solutions that connect efficient water infrastructure with renewable energy and agricultural expertise.

SOLAR PUMPING · GREENHOUSES · CONTROLLED ENVIRONMENTS
08

Landscape care & maintenance

Seasonal horticultural care, water audits and preventive equipment maintenance keep your landscape performing long after handover.

HORTICULTURE · PREVENTIVE CARE · WATER AUDITS
