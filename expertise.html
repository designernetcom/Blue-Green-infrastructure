ENGINEERING LIFECYCLE

Engineered before
it is built.

Every project moves through five disciplined phases, from hydraulic audit to long-term stewardship.

  1. 01

    Site assessment & hydraulic audit

    Topographic survey, water chemistry, soil percolation and municipal supply pressure audits.

  2. 02

    System engineering & architectural integration

    CAD schematic design, pipe sizing, dynamic pumping head calculations, electrical load profiling and control network layout.

  3. 03

    Sourcing, quality control & logistics

    Certified components from global OEM partners and regional trade networks, consolidated through the central Pune warehouse.

  4. 04

    Civil execution, installation & commissioning

    Earthworks, trenching, electro-fusion jointing, pump house assembly, panel wiring, hydrostatic testing and calibration.

  5. 05

    Stewardship & asset maintenance

    Preventive maintenance, seasonal horticultural care, system performance audits and continuous component servicing.
