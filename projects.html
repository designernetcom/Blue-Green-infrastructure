Ratan Tata Memorial Water Curtain

Location
Tata Motors plant, Pimpri-Chinchwad, Pune
Partners
Blue & Green founding partners
Executing firm
Blue & Green Infrastructure
Completed
Unveiled 28 Dec 2025

On the birth anniversary of the late Ratan Tata, a life-size statue was unveiled at Tata Motors’ Pimpri-Chinchwad plant. Blue & Green envisioned, designed and executed the illuminated water curtain that frames it.

  • Unveiling mechanismCustom motorised curtain system, 5.0 m high × 4.5 m wide, lifting three heavy panels at once from a single remote control
  • Water curtainRecirculating laminar veil with submerged high-lumen LED backlighting and a bubbler nozzle array at its base
  • Hydraulic circuitClosed-loop filtration bank with silent submersible pumps for low splash and acoustic clarity

Adani Data Centre Campuses

Location
Navi Mumbai ×2 · Hyderabad ×2
Architect
Ar. Gautami Renuse, The Thin Architect
Executing firm
Blue & Green Infrastructure
Role
Landscape & irrigation design consultancy

Data centre campuses extending to 170.17 acres, with multiple data centre buildings, a DG building, HSD yard and associated services. Blue & Green works to the architectural master plan, issuing landscape and irrigation packages at each design gate.

  • Water accountabilityDemonstrably efficient, low-draw irrigation for facilities scrutinised on water use
  • Security zoningPlanting that respects sightlines, perimeter security and controlled access
  • Service coordinationIrrigation routes resolved against DG buildings, HSD yards, cable trenches and utility corridors
