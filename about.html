ABOUT BLUE & GREEN

One integrated partner,
from first vision to lasting value.

A new firm, established in December 2025, built on the decades of delivery behind its three partners’ firms.

Blue & Green Infrastructure was established on 18 December 2025 in Pune, Maharashtra, to meet growing demand in environmental engineering, outdoor living, smart water management and architectural water features.

Developers, municipal authorities, industrial groups and architects have long had to engage separate vendors for water engineering, material sourcing, civil hardscape, softscape, fountains and maintenance. Blue & Green replaces that fragmentation with a single-window, Design–Supply–Build–Maintain model.

The firm unites three established market forces under one roof:

  • Agrotech Engineers & ConsultantsCentral warehousing hub and manufacturer-backed distribution network · Mr. Anil S. Pund
  • Aditi Irrigation TechnologiesLarge-project execution capability and international authorisations · Mr. Pramod Ballal
  • Samruddhi EnterprisesWholesale procurement and regional trade network · Mr. Rahul Kshirsagar
