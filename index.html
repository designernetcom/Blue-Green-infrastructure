About
Blue & Green

Blue & Green Infrastructure was established in Pune on 18 December 2025 as a single-window partner for landscape engineering, smart water management and architectural water features.

It brings together three founding partners and the established firms they lead: technical design and industrial supply, large-scale landscape execution, and regional trade procurement.

From the first design conversation to long-term care, we bring every part of your landscape together.
