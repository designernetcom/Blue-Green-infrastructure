The journey

Raised in an agricultural family in Ahilyanagar district, Western Maharashtra, Mr. Pund completed his B.Tech in Agricultural Engineering at Mahatma Phule Krishi Vidyapeeth (MPKV), Rahuri, between 1986 and 1990.

He joined Jain Irrigation Systems Ltd. as a Design Engineer directly after graduating. The role took him across Maharashtra and gave him a site-hardened command of installation, execution and commissioning under real field conditions.

The firm

Founded in 1996, Agrotech marks its 30th anniversary in 2026. Co-managed with Mrs. Anjali Pund, it runs a retail outlet in Shukrawar Peth, the heart of Pune’s irrigation and agriculture market, and a 2,500 sq. ft. warehouse on the city’s outskirts.

Agrotech is an authorised distributor for Rain Bird International. Its sister company, Yash Enterprises, is a channel partner for Jain Irrigation Systems, AMIAD Filtration (Israel), Finolex Plasson, Rivulis, Hunter Industries (USA) and WILO Pumps.

Contribution to Blue & GreenAgrotech’s central warehousing hub and distribution network, supported by thirty years of design and execution for corporate, industrial, defence and institutional clients.

The journey

A civil engineer from the University of Pune, Mr. Ballal established his proprietary firm, M/s. Ballal Sons, in 1999 to work across agriculture and landscaping.

He served as a distributor for Rain Bird from 2007 to 2017 and for Hunter Industries from 2018 to the present. That work took him to Singapore, the United States, the UAE and Europe, alongside international architects and landscape designers.

The firms

Aditi Irrigation Technologies, co-founded with Director Mrs. Aditi Ballal, has grown from an irrigation and fountain specialist into a full landscape execution house: hardscape, water bodies, drainage, lighting, lake management and play equipment, delivered across India.

Aditi holds Hunter Industries (USA) authorisation to design, sell and service across 19 states of North and North-East India. It is OASE Germany’s appointed India project partner for fountain technology and represents Aqua Master (USA) and Kompan (Denmark).

Hydroscape Engineering provides feasibility studies, landscape MEP design, quantity surveying, tender documentation and project management. It has 40 professionals, a principal design centre in Pune and offices in Mumbai, New Delhi and Bengaluru.

Contribution to Blue & GreenAditi’s large-project execution capability and international authorisations, with Hydroscape’s landscape MEP and PMC consultancy bridging architectural design and delivery.

The journey

Mr. Kshirsagar learned the irrigation business from the inside out at Veecas Enterprises, a pipe and irrigation trading firm, moving from warehouse keeper to accounts and then marketing. That progression gave him command of stock movement, margins and customer relationships as one discipline.

He is known in the market for his recall of price points and product specifications, and for his command of accounts and administration.

The firm

He established Samruddhi Enterprises in 2007 from an office in Shukrawar Peth, Pune, with a turnover of ₹27 lakh. The firm now turns over ₹12.5 crore, a nineteen-year record built on trade credibility.

Samruddhi delivers end-to-end drip, sprinkler and landscape irrigation, fountain and pool systems, mist systems, agricultural irrigation and water distribution networks.
